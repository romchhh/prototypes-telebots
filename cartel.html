Meet the company

In 2004 we launched our first gastronomy projects in Bukovel. Over 17 years CARTEL has opened 19 restaurants — Ukrainian, Italian, Asian and American cuisine — plus the hearty, affordable Eat’n’Go! quick-service chain. The Cartel family is present in Bukovel, Yaremche and Odesa. Every venue is known for excellent service, a distinctive atmosphere and outstanding food.


We chose not to stop at gastronomy. Today CARTEL is a major holding: hotels in Bukovel, real estate, the VODA club SPA complex, VERANDA lounge, authentic Wood-fired Banya and more. We keep growing to set a new standard of leisure in Ukraine.
